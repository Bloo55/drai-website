Support

Need help with DRAI? Email brandonloo.dev@gmail.com, or use Send Feedback in the app's Settings. DRAI is made by one developer, so a reply may take a few days.

Why isn't my AI brief updating?

AI features have daily limits that reset at midnight. If you've reached today's limit, the card says so. Until then you'll keep seeing your last brief, or the regular summary if there isn't one. If AI features are off, the dashboard always shows the regular summary.

How do I turn AI features on or off?

Open Settings in DRAI and find AI & Privacy, then use the Use AI Features switch. When it's off, nothing is sent to OpenAI, and your dashboard still works. The Privacy Policy explains what's shared when it's on.

Calendar, reminders, or weather aren't showing

Open the iPhone Settings app, tap Apps, then DRAI, and check that its permissions are allowed. Reopen DRAI and pull down on the dashboard to refresh.

How do I manage or cancel DRAI Pro?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. In DRAI, open Settings and tap Manage Subscription, or use your Apple Account subscriptions in the iPhone Settings app. Deleting DRAI doesn't cancel a subscription.

I bought DRAI Pro, but it isn't showing

Open Settings in DRAI and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the Apple Account you used to subscribe.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles refunds for purchases made through the App Store. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings in DRAI and tap Delete All DRAI Data. This clears what DRAI has saved on your iPhone and returns the app to setup.
