Privacy Policy

Effective date: [DATE YOU PUBLISH]

DRAI ("Daily Report AI") is an iPhone app made by Brandon Loo, a solo developer. This policy explains what the DRAI app and this website collect, where it goes, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • DRAI has no accounts. You never sign up or log in.
  • Most of your information (notes, memories, expenses, subscriptions, workouts, chat history, and settings) is stored only on your iPhone.
  • When you use AI features, DRAI sends your question and a summary of your day to DRAI's server, which passes it to OpenAI to write a reply.
  • DRAI does not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites.
  • You can delete everything DRAI stores on your iPhone at any time with Settings › Delete All DRAI Data.

Information stored on your iPhone

These stay on your device and are not sent to DRAI's server, except when they're included in an AI request (see the next section):

  • Notes, saved memories, expenses and budgets, subscriptions you add, workout logs and routines
  • Your AI chat history
  • Your settings: your name, your assistant's name and style, favorite teams, stock watchlist, commute destinations and Home location, and appearance choices
  • A background photo, if you choose one. DRAI saves a resized copy of only the photo you pick, using Apple's photo picker, so it never gets access to the rest of your library.

Calendar and Reminders. With your permission, DRAI reads and adds events and reminders using Apple's Calendar and Reminders. They stay in your Apple accounts. DRAI doesn't keep its own copy.

Location. With your permission, DRAI uses your location for local weather and travel times. Your location is not stored by DRAI or sent to DRAI's server.

Information that leaves your iPhone

DRAI only sends information off your device to make a feature work. DRAI's server runs on Cloudflare.

About the AI. DRAI asks OpenAI not to store conversations (the API's store option is set to false). OpenAI still processes each request to write the reply and handles data under its own policies. DRAI's server doesn't save your questions or the AI's answers.

Like most internet services, the providers above may receive technical information such as your IP address when your iPhone connects to them.

Subscriptions, usage limits, and your device ID

Device ID. The first time DRAI contacts its server, it creates a random ID for your installation. It isn't linked to your name, Apple ID, or phone number. DRAI's server uses it only to count AI messages and limit how fast requests are sent. It stores the counts for each ID, and the counts reset every day.

DRAI Pro. Purchases are handled by Apple. DRAI never sees your payment details. If you subscribe, the app sends your subscription's transaction ID to DRAI's server, which asks Apple whether it's active. The server keeps the transaction ID, its Pro status for a few hours, and up to five device IDs that use it, so your Pro limit works across your devices and reinstalls. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your iPhone's Settings, under your Apple ID.

This website

If you join the waitlist, your email address is stored with Brevo, the email service that sends DRAI launch and development updates. The sign-up form uses Google reCAPTCHA to block spam, which is covered by Google's own privacy policy. Your email is used only for DRAI updates and is never sold. Every email includes an unsubscribe link, or you can email me to be removed. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages.

What DRAI doesn't do

  • No accounts, ads, or advertising IDs
  • No selling or renting your information
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites
  • No analytics tools inside the app. If you've agreed to share analytics with Apple, Apple may send me anonymous crash reports.

Deleting your data

  • Settings › Delete All DRAI Data erases everything DRAI stores on your iPhone: notes, memories, expenses, subscriptions, workouts, chat history, settings, your background photo, widget data, and scheduled notifications. Your Apple Calendar and Reminders are not touched.
  • Deleting the app also removes its data from your iPhone.
  • To ask me to remove the usage counts or subscription records on DRAI's server, email me. Because DRAI has no accounts, include your subscription's Apple receipt or the date you subscribed so I can find them.

Children

DRAI isn't directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If DRAI starts collecting something new, for example if bank account connections are added, I'll update this policy and change the effective date before the feature launches.
