Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

DRAI is an iPhone app made by Brandon Loo, a solo developer. This policy explains what the DRAI app and the trydrai.app website do with your information. DRAI doesn't sell your information, doesn't show ads, and doesn't include third-party analytics or advertising tools.

Information that stays on your iPhone

Most of what you add to DRAI is stored on your iPhone, including your notes, saved memories, expenses, subscriptions, workouts, reminders you create, settings, and commute destinations. DRAI can also read your calendar and Apple Reminders if you give it permission. You can withdraw any permission in the iPhone Settings app.

Information sent when you use AI features

DRAI asks for your permission before using AI features. If you say no, none of the information below is sent to OpenAI, and you can change your answer anytime in the app's Settings under AI & Privacy.

When you use AI chat, Plan My Day, your daily brief, the Tomorrow preview, or the Weekly Recap summary, DRAI sends your request to its server, which passes it to OpenAI to create a response. To answer usefully, the request can include details from your daily report, such as:

  • Your message and your current date and time zone
  • Calendar events and reminders
  • Weather, commute, and leave-by information
  • News, stocks, and sports you follow
  • Spending, subscriptions, and workouts
  • Notes and saved memories

DRAI's server is not designed to save the content of these requests, and it asks OpenAI not to store them as saved responses. OpenAI handles this information under its own terms and privacy policy, and it may keep it for a limited time, for example to prevent abuse.

Other services DRAI uses

  • Cloudflare runs DRAI's server. It handles requests from the app, and it may keep short-term technical logs, such as request times, errors, and network information.
  • News and stocks: GNews and Finnhub provide headlines and stock quotes through DRAI's server, including the stock symbols you look up.
  • ESPN provides sports scores and schedules. The app connects to ESPN directly, so ESPN receives requests for the teams and leagues you view.
  • Apple provides weather, maps and commute times, Siri, subscriptions, and speech recognition. When you speak to DRAI, your voice may be processed by Apple to turn it into text.

Your location

If you allow it, DRAI uses your location while you're using the app to show local weather and commute times through Apple's services. Weather and commute details from your daily report, including destination names, can be included in AI requests as described above.

Usage limits and subscriptions

DRAI creates a random ID for each install and uses it to count daily usage, such as AI chat messages. This ID isn't tied to your name or email address. If you subscribe to DRAI Pro, the app also sends your subscription's transaction ID so DRAI can check with Apple that it's active and limit how many devices share it. Payments are handled entirely by Apple, and DRAI never sees your card details.

Website and waitlist

If you request early access on trydrai.app, the information you submit is collected through Formspree. It's used only for DRAI-related communication, including product updates and testing opportunities. It won't be sold, and you can ask to be removed at any time.

Deleting your information

Use Delete All DRAI Data in the app's Settings to clear your DRAI data from your iPhone. Deleting the app also removes the data it stores on your iPhone. Deleting DRAI doesn't cancel a subscription, which you manage in your Apple account. To ask about or remove information held outside your iPhone, contact us at the address below.

Children

DRAI isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If DRAI's data practices change, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
